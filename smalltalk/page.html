Activities

  1. Activity 1Tell us about yourself

    1. Say your name
    2. Share one fact about yourself(job / hobby / hometown / etc.)
    3. Tell us about one thing that made you happy this week

  2. Activity 2Make small talk

    Choose your role:

    Stayer
    • Stay where you are
    • Don’t read your card

    Let your partner start the conversation. Respond and support it

    After each 3-minute conversation:

    1. Swap cards with your partner
    2. Stay where you are and wait for a new partner
    Mover
    • Read your card and go up to a stayer
    • Use the idea on your card to start a conversation

    After each 3-minute conversation:

    1. Swap cards with your partner
    2. Move to the next stayer

  3. Activity 3Find 3 things in common

    1. Work in groups with people you don’t know well
    2. Find 3 things you all have in common(hobbies / habits / favorite places / etc.)
    3. Ask follow-up questions and talk a little more about each one

Small talk Toolkit

Conversation tips

Don’t be afraid to say hello first.

  • Open with a simple question. Listen to the answer and ask follow-up questions. When a topic interests you both, talk about it a little more.
  • Look for something you have in common. Compare your experiences and share a few details.
  • Not sure what to talk about? Try one of the safe topics.

If you’d like to stay in touch, suggest swapping contact info.

Conversation starter ideas

  • Introduce yourself directlyHi, I’m Kate. I don’t think we’ve met.
  • Just ask how they areHey, how are you doing today?
  • Talk about the weatherIt’s surprisingly warm today, don’t you think?
  • Give a complimentI love your earrings!
  • Use the contextHave you been to this café before?
  • Ask for their opinionDo you think the pancakes here are good?
  • Ask for a recommendationDo you know any good parks around here? I’d like to go for a short walk after the meetup.
  • Ask about something they shared earlierYou mentioned you’re learning Georgian. How’s it going?
  • Mention something you have in commonI’ve heard you live in Saburtalo. Me too!
  • Share something about your dayYou know, I’ve had a crazy morning. How about you?
  • Ask about holiday plansDo you already know what you’re gonna do on New Year’s Eve?
  • Share some newsHave you heard about the Thirty Seconds to Mars concert next year?
